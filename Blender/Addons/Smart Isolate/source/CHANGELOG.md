# Changelog — Smart Isolate

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20

- First release. Port of the ST3E 3ds Max Smart Isolate, replacing Local View
  on Numpad / and /.
- Nested isolation: a subset of the isolated objects isolates deeper; same
  selection steps back one level; nothing selected exits. All three choices
  are preferences, plus a non-nested 3ds Max mode.
- Keep Cameras / Keep Lights in isolation.
- The view does not move on enter (Frame Selection opt-in) or exit (Keep View
  on Exit).
- "Isolated N" header indicator; click to exit.
